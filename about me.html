About the Editor

The voice behind this heraldic and genealogical chronicle.

Hi, I'm Jack Savage, editor of this almanac. I'm a university computer science student with a long-standing fascination for heraldry, genealogy, rank, and titles.

This project is my way of preserving family history in a format that is both elegant and practical: easy to update, easy to browse, and built for long-term archival use.

As the site grows, expect deeper family profiles, cleaner lineage navigation, and more historical notes.
